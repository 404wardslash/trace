3. Methodology

The assessment followed an industry-standard penetration testing methodology encompassing the following phases:

  • Reconnaissance & Information Gathering Passive and active enumeration of the target environment, including port scanning, service fingerprinting, DNS enumeration, and web technology identification.
  • Vulnerability Discovery Systematic identification of potential weaknesses through automated scanning and manual testing, cross-referenced against known vulnerability databases (CVE, NVD, Exploit-DB).
  • Exploitation Controlled exploitation of confirmed vulnerabilities to demonstrate real-world impact and establish a foothold. All exploitation was performed within the agreed scope and without causing service disruption.
  • Post-Exploitation & Privilege Escalation Assessment of the impact achievable from an initial foothold, including local privilege escalation, credential extraction, and lateral movement within the network.
  • Documentation & Reporting All findings were documented with reproduction steps, screenshots, and prioritised remediation recommendations tailored to the organisation's risk profile.
